From Clay to Kiln

The full procedure, start to finish. Six steps, no shortcuts, one brick.

LIVE STAGE

RAW CLAY

STEP 01

Clay Extraction

Sourced from a single quarry bed, one layer down, chosen for consistency of mineral content rather than any romantic story we could tell about it.

STEP 02

Mixing & Tempering

Water is worked into the clay until it holds together without cracking, and air pockets are pressed out by hand — the difference between a brick and a small explosion in the kiln.

STEP 03

Pressed Into Form

The tempered clay is pressed into a single mold and struck level. It's the only design decision on this entire project we genuinely agonized over.

STEP 04

Air-Dried, Unbothered

Left to rest for several days until most of the moisture has left the clay. Fire it too wet and it cracks. So it waits, same as anything worth doing properly.

STEP 05

Kiln No. 7, 1,000°C

Fired for roughly twelve hours. The iron in the clay oxidizes under heat and turns the surface from dull grey to the terracotta red you're looking at now. That part, at least, is genuine chemistry.

STEP 06

Cooled & Inspected

Left to cool slowly, then checked for cracks, chips, and general demeanor. Approved. Catalogued as Exhibit No. 001. Placed, finally, under glass.

Provenance & Specifications

Rotate freely. Examine every angle. It is, from all sides, a brick — and we stand by that.
